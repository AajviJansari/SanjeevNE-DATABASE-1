About SanjeevNE-DB

The SanjeevNE-DB is comprehensive database that integrates electronic, structural and physicochemical properties of phytoconstituents, enabling computational analysis for drug discovery through quantum chemistry and molecular modeling. It has been developed under Centre of Excellence at NIPER Guwahati focusing on abundant phytoconstituents of medicinal plant in particular states of North East India.It is freely available to the academic research groups, however, the industries/private groups will be charged a licensing fees.

Beneficial for :

  • Academics and Research Groups
  • Phytopharmaceutical Companies
  • Drug Discovery Industries
  • Herbal Cosmetic Industries
